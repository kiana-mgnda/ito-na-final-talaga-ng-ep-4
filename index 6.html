Episode 04 - Whenever You Are Sad

Hello Chelle,

If you're reading this letter, I'm guessing you're having one of those days na feel mo it's a little too much. Maybe ngayon overwhelmed ka, pagod, or simply tired and ngayon din yung day na wala ka na ding energy to makipag usap sa iba. Kung ano man yung reason, I want you to know na it's okay na magkaroon ka ng bad day. Hindi mo kailangang maging strong, happy, or okay all of the time kasi merong talagang time na it's okay to cry, be sad, or magkaroon ng dark days.

Sometimes nagpu-put tayo ng pressure sa sarili natin para ma-figure out saan tayo nagkakamali. Nagwo-worry tayo about school, sa family, friends, and even yung mga things na hindi pa naman nangyayari. Pero kahit ganon, hindi mo siya kailangang i-solve kaagad. Okay lang mag-pause muna. Okay lang mag-rest. Okay lang na sabihin mong, "Pagod na ako." Hindi ibig sabihin ng "teka muna, pagod na ako" eh you are giving up. Sometimes you are just giving yourself time to breathe and giving yourself time to continue.

One bad day doesn't define your whole life.

Whatever problem you're facing right now, if it's heavy or a simple problem, don't forget na lilipas din yan and things will get better. Plus, don't be too harsh to yourself ha. Take your time to breathe, unwind, and rest. Give yourself the kindness na binibigay mo sa loved ones mo.

Lagi kang nandiyan tuwing meron ako bad and good days, and gusto ko ring sabihin sayo na nandito lang rin ako para sayo!

You don't have to be okay before coming to me. Wag mong isipin na hindi kita dadamayan, pwede kang pumunta exactly as you are.

When the good days come, I hope you'll look back and say na, "Yes, nalampasan ko," and ma-realize mo na na-defeat mo yung challenges na finace mo.

Chelle, if hindi mo day ngayon, okay lang yan. Meron pa namang bukas para mag-start ulit ng panibago. Take each thing one step at a time. And remember, we love you, we appreciate you, and we are always here for you.

And if no one has told you lately, I'm super duper proud of you! I'm proud na you are continuing. I'm proud of you trying. I'm proud of the person you are becoming.

I'll be one of the people cheering for you, supporting you, and ireremind kita always na you are loved!

I LOVE YOU! 💗

One more thing...
